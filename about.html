Our purpose

Turn marketplace complexity into confident decisions

Every e-commerce order creates a trail of sales, returns, RTO, fees, taxes, deductions and settlements. When these numbers live in separate files, real profit becomes difficult to find.

Queshift brings that trail together. Built by AW Taxation, the software combines practical accounting knowledge with a reconciliation workflow focused on order-wise calculation.
